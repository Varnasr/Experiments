Two conversions that survey and budget work needs constantly. The first restates a rupee amount at the prices of another month or year, using the all-India Consumer Price Index. The second turns rupees into 2021 international dollars and back, the unit in which the World Bank's poverty lines are set.

Method and limits

An amount at the prices of period A is restated at the prices of period B by multiplying by the ratio of the price index in B to the index in A. A calendar year uses the average of its twelve monthly indices, and a financial year the average from April to the following March. A year with a month missing is not offered.

The index is the all-India Consumer Price Index, rural and urban combined, general. The series with base 2012 runs from January 2013 to December 2025. From January 2026 the Ministry publishes a new series with base 2024 and a different basket. The two overlap for the twelve months of 2025, and this page joins them at the ratio of their 2025 averages, . The month-by-month ratio in 2025 runs from 1.894 to 1.903, so a conversion that crosses January 2026 carries an uncertainty of about a quarter of one per cent from the join alone.

A 2021 PPP dollar is a rupee amount first restated at 2021 prices and then divided by the World Bank's 2021 conversion factor for India: . The household consumption factor is the one behind the international poverty lines of $3.00, $4.20 and $8.30 a day. It is a national average. It does not know that rural prices are lower than urban ones or that Bihar is cheaper than Kerala.

The general index is an average basket. A household that spends most of its money on food faces food inflation, which this index understates in some years and overstates in others. The index before 2013 is not included, and neither are the rural, urban or state series.

Sources: Consumer Price Index, Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, through the MoSPI data service, read on 11 November 2026. World Bank, World Development Indicators, series PA.NUS.PRVT.PP and PA.NUS.PPP, last updated 8 October 2026.
