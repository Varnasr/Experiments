# Land Acquisition Compensation Calculator

Live at **https://varnasr-experiments.netlify.app/land-acquisition/**

Compensation and rehabilitation entitlements under the Right to Fair Compensation
and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013,
computed line by line with the section or schedule item cited beside each.

| Line | Rule | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market value | the higher of the Stamp Act circle rate, the average of the top 50% of sale deeds for similar land in the vicinity over three years, and any consented amount | section 26(1); First Schedule serial 1 |
| Multiplication factor | rural land × 1.00 to 2.00 as notified by the state by distance from the urban area; urban × 1 | section 26(2); First Schedule serial 2 |
| Assets attached | buildings, trees, standing crops, wells as valued | section 29; serial 3 |
| Solatium | 100% of (market value × factor + assets) | section 30(1); serial 4 |
| Additional amount | 12% a year on the market value from the section 4(2) notification to the award or possession, whichever is earlier | section 30(3) |
| Rehabilitation and resettlement | house (rural: constructed house; urban: 50 sq m or ₹1,50,000 in lieu); employment, ₹5,00,000 or ₹2,000 a month for 20 years indexed; subsistence ₹3,000 a month for a year (plus ₹50,000 for SC/ST in Scheduled Areas); transportation ₹50,000; cattle shed or petty shop ₹25,000; artisan ₹25,000; resettlement allowance ₹50,000; land for land in irrigation projects; stamp duty borne by the requiring body | Second Schedule items 1 to 10 |

The annuity option is shown as a present value on a discount rate and CPI
indexation the reader sets, next to its nominal total. A note compares the same
inputs under the 1894 Act (30% solatium, no factor, no schedule).

## Where the page stops

It applies the central Act's minimums and the factor you enter. States have
amended the Act, exempted project categories, notified their own factors and
raised Second Schedule amounts; whether the 12% addition runs on the bare market
value or the multiplied value is not uniform across awards, so the page offers
both. Sections 41 and 42 (Scheduled Areas), section 46 (private purchase
threshold), section 80 (interest on delay) and the income-tax position beyond the
section 96 exemption are outside it.

## Verification

Every First Schedule line was reproduced by hand on the default inputs (market
value ₹38,40,000 for 1.2 ha at ₹32,00,000; × 1.5; + ₹4,00,000; solatium equal to
that sum; 12% for 776 days on ₹38,40,000 = ₹9,79,673), and the Second Schedule
amounts were checked against the schedule text.
